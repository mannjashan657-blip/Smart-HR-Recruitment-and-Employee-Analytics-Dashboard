
# Smart HR Recruitment and Employee Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR analytics dashboard built to help organizations track recruitment metrics, employee attrition, job satisfaction, and workforce distribution across departments, roles, and demographics.


📊 Overview

This dashboard consolidates key HR metrics into a single view, enabling HR teams and decision-makers to monitor employee experience, satisfaction, and attrition trends at a glance. It combines KPI cards, charts, and a hierarchical filter tree to support both high-level summaries and drill-down analysis.

✨ Key Features

- **Hierarchical Filter Panel** — Slice data by Gender, Department, and Job Role for granular analysis.
- **KPI Cards** — Quick-glance metrics including Employee Experience, Remaining Score, and Average Employee tenure/count.
- **Attrition vs. Job Satisfaction** — Scatter plot comparing Sum of Monthly Income and Sum of Years at Company, segmented by attrition status (Yes/No) across satisfaction levels.
- **Job Satisfaction Distribution** — Bar chart showing total employees across Very High, High, Low, and Medium satisfaction levels.
- **Marital Status Breakdown** — Pie chart of employees by Married, Single, and Divorced status.
- **Department Headcount** — Stacked bar chart of employee counts by Work-Life Balance rating (Bad, Best, Better, Good) across Research & Development, Sales, and Human Resources.
- **Monthly Income by Job Role** — Bar chart comparing average/sum income across roles like Sales, Manager, Research, Manufacturing, Healthcare, Laboratory, and Human Resources.
- **Education Level Distribution** — Donut chart segmenting employees by Bachelor's, Master's, College, and Below College education levels.

🧩 Dashboard Sections

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| Filter Tree | Left-hand panel for filtering by Gender → Department → Job Role |
| KPI Summary | Top-row scorecards for core metrics |
| Attrition Analysis | Scatter plot correlating income, tenure, attrition, and satisfaction |
| Satisfaction & Demographics | Job satisfaction, marital status, and education level charts |
| Departmental View | Employee distribution by department and work-life balance |
| Compensation View | Monthly income breakdown by job role |
